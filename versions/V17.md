## Key features

- **Offline foundation:** IndexedDB storage powered by Dexie.js for contacts, products, and invoices.
- **BWA and financial reporting:** Income statements, VAT calculations, and initial accounting exports.
- **Multilingual foundation:** Localization support for English, German, French, and Spanish.
- **Audio feedback:** Synthesized sounds for clicks, notifications, and confirmations.
