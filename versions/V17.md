## 🌟 Key Features of Version 17

- **Foundation Offline Architecture:** IndexedDB database using Dexie.js for client-side storage of contacts, products, and invoices.
- **BWA & Financial Reporting:** Income statements, VAT calculations, and initial accounting exports.
- **Multilingual Foundation:** English, German, French, and Spanish localization support.
- **Audio Feedback:** Synthesized sound effects for clicks, notifications, and confirmations.
