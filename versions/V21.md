## 🌟 Version 21.10.0 Updates (Master Volume Slider, Multi-Sound Audio Library, Categorized Settings Hub & Custom Language Pack Engine)

### 1. Master Volume Control Slider ("Schöner Schieberegler")
- Implemented an elegant, responsive volume range slider in Settings under "Sound & Audio" with dynamic icons (`VolumeX`, `Volume1`, `Volume2`), real-time percentage indicators (`0%` to `100%`), and 5 quick preset pill selectors (`15%`, `35%`, `60%`, `85%`, `100%`).
- Added a dedicated "Lautstärke probehören" button to instantly audition current gain levels without leaving the slider.
- Connected the slider to Web Audio API gain scaling in `src/lib/sound.ts` (`sounds.getVolume()`, `sounds.setVolume()`), controlling master volume across all synthesized sound effects with persistent localStorage storage.

### 2. Enriched Sound Effects Library & Interactive Sound Matrix
- Synthesized and introduced rich procedural sound effects across key enterprise workflows:
  - **Kasse, Bezahlung & Geldverkehr**: Payment Success chord (`playPaymentSuccess`), Cash Drawer mechanical roll and latch (`playCashDrawer`), Metallic Coin Clink (`playCoinClink`), Contactless NFC Terminal double-beep (`playNfcBeep`), Desk Bell (`playBell`), and classic Cash Register Ka-Ching (`playKaChing`).
  - **Geschäftsprozesse, Termine & Belege**: Calendar 2-way sync confirmation tone (`playCalendarSync`), Success Fanfare chord progression (`playFanfare`), Outgoing Invoice Send glide whoosh (`playSendInvoice`), and Trash Empty paper rustle (`playTrashEmpty`).
  - **Benutzeroberfläche & Fenstersteuerung**: Subtle Click, Pop, Success, Error, Window Lock (`playLock`), and Window Unlock (`playUnlock`).
- Built an interactive categorized sound preview matrix directly in Settings > Sound & Audio with play triggers, descriptions, and category tags.
- Integrated sound effects into live POS checkouts and the Payment Modal for authentic multi-phase auditory feedback on card and cash payments.

### 3. Categorized Settings Navigation & Hub Architecture
- Overhauled `SettingsModule.tsx` navigation from a flat list into 4 clean, structured categories:
  1. **Übersicht & Schnellzugriff**: Startseite
  2. **Unternehmen & Finanzen**: Allgemein & Stammdaten, Rechnungs-Layout & Nummernkreise, Verbindungen & Kalender, Automatische Datensicherung
  3. **Oberfläche & Personalisierung**: Erscheinungsbild, Sprache & Region, Sound & Audio
  4. **System & Geräte**: Windows Desktop-App, Entwickler & Schnittstellen, Info & Lizenzen
- Created a visual "Einstellungsbereiche & Module" dashboard hub on the Settings Home tab displaying direct navigation cards for all modules with item badges, status indicators, and hover elevation.
- Upgraded the mobile/compact carousel switcher to group modules by category with responsive horizontal scrolling.

### 4. Custom JSON Language Pack Engine & Translation Template
- Added full external language customization capabilities allowing businesses to translate SOCDOF into any language or customize industry-specific terminology:
  - **Download JSON Translation Template**: Generates a clean `.json` file containing all English master translation keys for custom translation.
  - **Export Current Language**: 1-click export of any active built-in language pack (`de.json`, `en.json`, `fr.json`, `es.json`).
  - **Import Language Pack**: File uploader parsing, validating, and persisting custom translation packs into IndexedDB/localStorage.
  - **Installed Packs Management**: Active status indicator card, 1-click activation/deactivation, and deletion of custom packs.
  - **Windows Desktop Offline Folder Guidance**: Added instructions for placing `.json` translation files directly in `%APPDATA%/socdof/languages/` for packaged desktop deployments.
