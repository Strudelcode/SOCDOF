## 🌟 Key Features of Version 18

- **Multi-Window Desktop Workspace:** Windows 11 style floating windows with Aero Snap, titlebar controls, minimizing to taskbar, maximizing, and Z-index management.
- **DIN 5008 & Modern Invoicing:** Professional invoice creation, fold marks, dynamic QR-GiroCode payment generation, and high-resolution PDF export.
- **Fast Touch POS & Kitchen Display (KDS):** Point of Sale module with barcode scanner support, rapid item search, and receipt printing.
- **Double-Entry Inventory & Stock Moves:** Real-time stock movement tracking, incoming goods, and inventory valuation.
- **Accompanying Native Installer (.EXE):** Setup builds packaged with NSIS and Electron for 100% offline standalone execution.
- **Theme Customizer & Picasso Palette:** Dynamic accent color system with real-time CSS variable binding across dark and light modes.
